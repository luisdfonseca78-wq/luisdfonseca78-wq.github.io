Aerospace Engineering @ UC Berkeley

Focus: Electric Propulsion, Space Systems Engineering, Space Robotics & Control Systems

I am an Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at UC Berkeley driven by a deep passion for pushing the boundaries of space exploration and flight hardware. My work focuses on solving complex challenges in electric propulsion, space robotics, and autonomous systems through hands-on development and modeling.

Technical Skills: Python, MATLAB, Jupyter, TensorFlow, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, AutoCAD, Ansys, 3D Printing, WaterJet & Laser Cutting.

Research & Publications

Nanotechnology Concepts Lab

Sustainable Aviation Systems Lab

Evaluated alternative-fuel flight trajectories and developed machine learning models to analyze fuel consumption, emissions, and environmental impact using NASA trajectory datasets.

Squishy Robotics / BEST Lab

Designed and tested pan-tilt actuation systems using motor drives and encoders. Evaluated drivetrain gear ratios and integrated sensor arrays with ML teams for robotic data collection.

AIAA & Conference Papers
